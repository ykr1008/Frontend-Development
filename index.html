Introduction

Renewable energy comes from natural sources that are continuously replenished, such as sunlight, wind, flowing water, and geothermal heat. These energy sources can reduce dependence on fossil fuels and help create a more sustainable energy system.

Benefits of Renewable Energy

Renewable energy can provide environmental, economic, and social benefits. Unlike finite fossil fuel resources, many renewable sources can be replenished naturally.

  • Reduces greenhouse gas emissions
  • Decreases air pollution
  • Promotes energy independence
  • Creates jobs in new industries

Challenges of Renewable Energy

Although renewable energy offers many advantages, its adoption also presents challenges. Solar and wind power depend on weather conditions, while large-scale renewable projects can require significant investment and infrastructure.

Energy storage is another important challenge because electricity generated from renewable sources may need to be stored for use when sunlight or wind is unavailable.

Did You Know?

The International Energy Agency (IEA) reported that renewable energy sources accounted for nearly 30% of global electricity generation in 2020, and this share is expected to grow in the coming years.
